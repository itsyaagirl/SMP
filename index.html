Hi, I'm Reva — a Seattle-based creator making scroll-stopping short-form content across cafés, tech, beauty, fashion, and lifestyle luxury. My community leans into cozy-meets-elevated: the perfect matcha spot, the gadget worth the hype, the get-ready-with-me, and the little luxuries that make everyday life feel intentional.

Because I create across so many verticals, I can meet almost any brand where its audience already is — whether that's a rainy-day café aesthetic or an unboxing that actually converts.
